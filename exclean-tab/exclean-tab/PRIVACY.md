# Exclean Tab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Exclean Tab replaces the Chrome new tab page. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Settings** (theme, appearance, shape, shortcuts you add, bookmarks bar visibility) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync`. They stay in your browser and, if you have Chrome sync enabled, in your own Google account. The developer has no access to them.
- **Bookmarks** are read to display the bookmarks bar on the new tab page. They are never modified, copied elsewhere or sent anywhere.
- **Site icons** for bookmarks are read from Chrome's local favicon cache.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not read, modify or delete browsing history.
- It does not make network requests of its own. The only navigation it performs is opening the search query you type on google.com, or opening a bookmark or shortcut you click.
- It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address listed on the Chrome Web Store page of this extension.
